## 🎯 Key Business Objectives

* **Sales Performance Tracking:** Identify top-performing product categories, peak sales hours, and monthly revenue trends.
* **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Evaluate average order value (AOV), total order volume, and item combination trends.
* **Store Location Comparison:** Analyze sales variance across different store locations to optimize inventory and staffing.
* **Product Analytics:** Track performance metrics for coffee, tea, bakery items, and merchandise.

## 📊 Key Insights & Analytics Summary

1. **Peak Operating Hours:** Highest footfall and revenue are generated during morning peak hours (8:00 AM – 11:00 AM).
2. **Top Revenue Categories:** Coffee and Espresso-based beverages contribute the largest percentage of overall gross revenue.
3. **Location Strategy:** Core urban store locations outperform suburban outlets in daily transaction volume.
